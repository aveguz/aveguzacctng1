Context · Service Business

For this module, we stay with a service business.

We will use Power Laundry, a sample service business. Its transactions and Chart of Accounts provide one consistent setting for learning the journalizing process.

Sample Business: Power Laundry

Power Laundry receives owner investment, purchases equipment and supplies, provides laundry services, collects from customers, pays liabilities, and pays operating expenses.

Our boundary for now
