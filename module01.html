Overview · Context

Where these transactions happen

Business transactions vary with the nature of the business.

Service and merchandising businesses share many common transactions, but merchandising businesses also have transactions that relate specifically to buying and selling goods.
For now, we will focus on service business transactions. These give us a clear foundation for analyzing changes in the Expanded Accounting Equation. Merchandising transactions will be examined separately in a later lesson.

Our focus in this module

Owner investment, purchasing assets, services for cash or on account, collections, payments, borrowing, withdrawals, and a few compound transactions.

Not included yet: adjusting entries and merchandising-specific transactions.
