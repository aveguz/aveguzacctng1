02 · What It Is

One list. Two columns. One important check.

A trial balance is a listing of the accounts in the General Ledger together with their respective Debit or Credit balances at a particular date.

05 · Check

What if it doesn't balance?

If Debit and Credit totals do not agree, check systematically instead of guessing.

1. Re-add both columns

A simple footing error may have occurred.

2. Check each balance

An account may have been omitted, copied incorrectly, or placed on the wrong side.

3. Trace to the ledger

Compare each Trial Balance account with its source balance one by one.

Balanced does not always mean correct.Equal Debit and Credit totals show arithmetic equality, but they do not prove every transaction was recorded or classified correctly.
