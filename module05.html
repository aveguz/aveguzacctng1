Context

Accounting needs order before it can record.

Knowing that an account increased or decreased is only part of the process. We also need a consistent way to organize accounts and record their changes.

Why classify accounts?

Account classification tells us what an account represents: an Asset, Liability, Owner's Equity account, Revenue, Expense, or Withdrawal. This keeps the accounting system consistent and easier to verify.

Why normal balances?

An account's normal balance is the side where increases are normally recorded. Once the classification is known, the Debit–Credit rule becomes much more logical.

05 · Debit / Credit Framework

Normal side = increase side

Once the normal balance is known, the rule follows: increases go to the normal side; decreases go to the opposite side.

Do not memorize Debit = increase and Credit = decrease.

Instead ask: What type of account is this? → What is its normal side? → Is the account increasing or decreasing?
