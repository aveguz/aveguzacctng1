01 · Purpose

Why do we post?

The General Journal tells us what happened in chronological order. The General Ledger reorganizes that information by account.

General Journal

Organized by date

Useful when we ask: What happened first? What happened next?

General Ledger

Organized by account

Useful when we ask: What happened to Cash, Accounts Payable, or Service Revenue?

Traditional term to rememberThe ledger is traditionally called the book of final entry. More practically, it is where transactions are classified and accumulated by account.
Core rulePosting does not decide Debit or Credit again. A Debit in the journal remains a Debit in the ledger; a Credit remains a Credit.

Running Balance Reminder · 101 Cash

Previous Balance + Debit − Credit = New Balance

Because Cash normally has a Debit balance, a Debit posting increases its running balance and a Credit posting decreases it.

Jan 01₱0 + ₱70,000 = ₱70,000 Dr
Jan 03₱70,000 − ₱5,000 = ₱65,000 Dr
Jan 09₱37,000 + ₱10,000 = ₱47,000 Dr

For accounts that normally carry a Credit balance, keep the account's normal balance in mind when interpreting increases and decreases.

Notice the totals.Across the ledger formats, 101 Cash has ₱85,000 total Debits and ₱40,000 total Credits. These totals lead directly to footing the ledger and determining the account balance.
The format changed. The accounting did not. All three presentations describe the same Cash account and postings.

F / FolioTraditional label in manual ledger forms.
Ref.General reference label used by many forms.
PRPosting Reference. In our General Journal, this carries the COA account number.
Forms can differ.In some manual bookkeeping systems or textbooks, the journal posting-reference column may instead show a ledger page or folio reference. For our current modules, we will keep the convention you already know: journal PR = COA account number, while the ledger F/Ref. = source journal such as GJ-01.
Look Ahead · Other source journals

For now, we use the General Journal (GJ). Later, a business may use journals such as a Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ) or Cash Disbursements Journal (CDJ); the ledger reference identifies the source used.

Why this mattersClear references make it possible to trace a ledger balance back to its journal source.
07 · Footing and Account Balance

Before you calculate, recall the normal balance.

Footing is the traditional term for totaling the Debit and Credit money columns. The difference gives the account balance.

101 Cash · AssetNormal Balance: Debit
Total Debit postings₱85,000
Total Credit postings₱40,000
₱85,000 − ₱40,000 = ₱45,000 Debit Balance
Keep the normal balance in mind.For a liability such as 201 Accounts Payable, you would ordinarily expect a Credit balance instead.
Manual vs. digital systemsPosting may be done daily or periodically in manual systems. In computerized systems, posting may occur automatically. The relationship remains: Journal → Ledger.
