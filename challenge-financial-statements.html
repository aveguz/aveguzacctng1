Quick Review

Before You Begin

Tap one option in each grid, then check your answers. Once you begin, the guide is no longer shown during the challenge.

Real / Permanent

Assets, contra-assets, liabilities, and owner's capital.

Balances carry forward to the next accounting period.

Nominal / Temporary

Revenues, expenses, and owner's drawing.

Balances are closed at the end of the accounting period.

Where does it go?

Financial statementAccounts to recognize
Income StatementRevenues and expenses
Statement of Financial Position (Balance Sheet)Assets, contra-assets, liabilities, and owner's capital
Statement of Changes in Owner's EquityOwner's drawing
Game convention: Choose the account's primary placement. Owner's Capital is classified under the Statement of Financial Position, although it is also reflected in the Statement of Changes in Owner's Equity. Owner's Drawing is placed in the Statement of Changes in Owner's Equity. Contra-assets reduce related assets and are permanent accounts. A name such as “Unearned Service Revenue” is a liability, not earned revenue.
