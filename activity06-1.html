Activity 06.1 · Power Laundry

Build the accounting record.

Student InformationRequired for your activity summary and proof of attempt.
Please complete your name, section, and date before starting the activity.

Power Laundry is a service business that provides laundry and related services to its customers. During the period, the business receives investments, earns service revenue, purchases assets and supplies, pays expenses and obligations, collects amounts from customers, and records owner withdrawals.

Your task is to analyze each transaction and record it properly in the General Journal using the accounts provided in the Chart of Accounts. Complete the Date, Particulars, PR, Debit, and Credit information for each entry.

A note on supplies: Supplies purchased for future use are recorded first as Laundry Supplies, an asset, rather than Laundry Supplies Expense. Both accounts appear in the Chart of Accounts, but their distinction becomes more important when adjusting entries are introduced later.
