Activity 07.1 · Power Laundry

Post the journal to the ledger.

Use the running General Journal produced in Activity 06.1 as the source for posting entries to the General Ledger accounts.

Activity 06.1 gave you a complete General Journal. In this activity, you will use that journal as the source record and post each entry to the correct ledger account.

The objective is not to re-create the journal. Instead, you will read each entry, identify the correct account, and transfer the debit or credit to the proper side of the ledger account using the proper reference.
